# Privacy Policy

Material Files (this fork) does **not** collect, store or transmit any personal data, analytics or crash reports to the developer or to any third party.

- No analytics or crash-reporting services (such as Google Analytics, Firebase or Crashlytics) are included in the app.
- All settings, bookmarks and server credentials you enter stay on your device.
- The app only connects to the network servers (FTP, SFTP, SMB, WebDAV, etc.) that you explicitly configure.

If you have questions, open an issue in this repository.
